Subscription businesses quietly lose money to failed payments, missed invoices, expired discounts and under-billing, and no team can audit every invoice by hand. I built an end-to-end platform that detects suspicious invoices, scores how likely each is to be leaking revenue, and explains why it was flagged.

  • Synthetic SaaS billing data
  • EDA
  • SQL analytics
  • Model building: Isolation Forest + XGBoost
  • Streamlit app
  • Power BI
6leakage patterns modelled
20+SQL queries, diagnostics to executive
2models, unsupervised + supervised
  • Streamlit app filters invoices, shows KPIs and scores a selected invoice live, with a plain-language reason for every flag.
  • Power BI executive dashboard with KPI cards, trends and a Pareto view of leakage causes.
  • Dataset is AI-assisted synthetic data with six built-in leakage patterns, not a real company’s records.
